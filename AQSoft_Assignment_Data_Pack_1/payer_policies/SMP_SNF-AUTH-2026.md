# Sunshine Medicaid Partners - Provider Bulletin SNF-AUTH-2026
## Prior Authorization for Initial Skilled Nursing Facility Visits

1. Purpose: to align physician billing with the plan's SNF admission authorization program.
2. Effective for dates of service ON OR AFTER 2026-04-01, initial nursing facility care (CPT 99304, 99305, 99306) requires the SNF admission authorization number to be reported on the professional claim.
3. Claims for dates of service before 2026-04-01 do not require an authorization number.
4. Missing authorization denies with CARC 197. Retro-authorization may be requested within 14 days of admission.
5. Appeals must be filed within 60 days of the denial date.
