# Northstar Health Plan - Reimbursement Policy HOSP-FREQ-07
## Subsequent Hospital Care - Frequency
Effective: 2025-10-01

1. Northstar reimburses one subsequent hospital care service (CPT 99231-99233) per patient, per calendar day, per specialty group.
2. When two practitioners of the same specialty group bill subsequent hospital care for the same patient on the same date, the second claim received will be denied (CARC 151).
3. Exception: a second visit on the same date is reimbursable when the medical record shows a significant change in condition and the second visit is billed with modifier 25 and a distinct diagnosis. Appeals must include both progress notes.
4. Appeals must be filed within 180 days of the denial date.
