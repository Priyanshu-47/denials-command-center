# Modifier 25 - Evaluation & Management on the Same Day as a Procedure
Applies to: Northstar Health Plan, Coastal Senior Advantage, Sunshine Medicaid Partners and Meridian PPO (all four payers follow the national NCCI edit)
Effective: 2026-01-01

1. An E/M service billed on the same date as a procedure by the same provider is considered included in the procedure unless the E/M line carries modifier 25.
2. Modifier 25 indicates a significant, separately identifiable E/M service. Documentation must support it.
3. Claims denied for this reason (CARC 97 / RARC M15) may be corrected and resubmitted as a replacement claim (frequency code 7) within each payer's corrected-claim window (see payer_rules.csv).
