# Coastal Senior Advantage - Provider Enrollment Requirement
Effective: 2025-07-01

1. A rendering practitioner must be enrolled with Coastal Senior Advantage, with an enrollment effective date on or before the date of service.
2. Services by practitioners not yet enrolled deny with CARC B7 (RARC N570).
3. Enrollment is not retroactive: services before the enrollment effective date are not payable and may not be appealed on that basis.
4. New practitioners should allow 60 to 90 days for enrollment to complete.
