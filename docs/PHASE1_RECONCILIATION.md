## Method notes

- Cash is measured from `BPR` at **transaction** level (one file carries 7–9 transaction sets, each with its own check date and often its own payer).
- `Σ CLP04` counts **every** `CLP` in an imported file, including payment events whose claim is not in the export, so the two sides of the reconciliation describe the same population.
- Adjustments are summed over the same population, and the portion that cannot be attributed to a claim is broken out rather than dropped.
- The duplicate resend is retained, flagged, and contributes **$0.00** to cash.
- Every monetary value is `decimal` with a fixed two-decimal scale; no floating point type appears anywhere in the money path.
